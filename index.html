Our Heritage

Thirty Years of
Culinary Excellence

The Grand Palace was born from a single vision: to create a dining destination worthy of Four Seasons’ uncompromising standard of luxury. Founded in 1994, the restaurant was conceived as a stage where the world’s greatest culinary talents perform at the highest level.

Over three decades, we have hosted royalty, heads of state, and the world’s most celebrated artists. Each visit is a chapter in a story of extraordinary hospitality — a story that continues to unfold with every plate we serve.

Executive Chef

Antoine Dubois

Born in Lyon and trained under the legendary Paul Bocuse, Chef Antoine Dubois brings thirty years of mastery to The Grand Palace kitchen. His philosophy: let the finest ingredients speak, then whisper poetry into each plate through technique and an obsessive pursuit of perfection.
